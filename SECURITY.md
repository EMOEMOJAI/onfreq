# Security & privacy

Report vulnerabilities through [GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/EMOEMOJAI/onfreq/security/advisories/new). Never include credentials or unredacted member records in public issues.

Keep deployment values in Wrangler secrets and ignored `.dev.vars`, `.envrc`, `wrangler.local.jsonc`, `*.local.sh` or `config/` files; only `config/optional-secrets.example` is public. Do not publish working-directory ZIPs; use a reviewed Git archive. Rotate any exposed credential at its provider.

The optional `POST /ivao/webhook` is authenticated by the IVAO (Hook0) HMAC signature keyed by `IVAO_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, is disabled while that secret is unset, shorter than 32 characters or equal to `POLL_SECRET`, `HISTORY_SECRET`, `IVAO_CLIENT_SECRET`, `DISCORD_BOT_TOKEN`, `GCA_ADMIN_HMAC_KEY` or `GCA_ADMIN_HMAC_KEY_NEXT`, and never stores or logs payloads.

`/poll` and `/health` require the `POLL_SECRET` bearer token; the `/gca-history` routes, including the operator's `/gca-history/records` and `/gca-history/records/delete`, require the separate `HISTORY_SECRET`. The optional [website-managed GCA records](docs/setup.md#website-managed-gca-records) routes (`PUT /gca/records`, `GET /gca/records/meta`, `POST /gca/records/history/purge`) require an HMAC-SHA256 signature keyed by `GCA_ADMIN_HMAC_KEY`, or `GCA_ADMIN_HMAC_KEY_NEXT` during a rotation, over the method, path, key id, timestamp, single-use nonce and body hash. The Worker verifies it before any Durable Object access, and the routes are disabled while the key is unset, shorter than 32 characters or reused. They have no CORS and never log keys, nonces, signatures or bodies. Use one key per deployment, since the signature does not cover the host name.

## Data retention

The bot processes IVAO and Discord IDs. Optional GCA reminder rows that were delivered, reserved, failed or capped (recorded without a member DM because of the 24-hour limit), with their member and connection identifiers, occurrence numbers and DM times, are kept indefinitely by design: they are what prevents a second reminder for the same connection. Stale pending, baseline and unmapped reminder rows, with their occurrences, are pruned 7 days after the connection was last seen by an enabled poll; the prune runs on every poll, even while GCA reminders are off or misconfigured. Staff-copy payloads, including those for capped occurrences, clear after delivery or handled terminal failure, but pending/reserved copies may retain them, including copies for a previous `GCA_COPY_USER_ID`, which are never sent. Only the manual [cleanup](docs/setup.md#private-history-maintenance) removes them: copies older than 30 days, and unsent copies for any account but the current one. Optional short-session reminders store the VID, IVAO session id and online times of every ATC member in the monitored division while they are online and for up to one hour after; their reminder records keep the member ID for seven days and the callsign until the reminder is finished or expires. Online records are cleared while the feature is off, and `SHORT_SESSION_DM_ENABLED="purge"` drops all of it on the next successful poll (logged as `short_session_purged`). Discord rate-limit cooldowns are kept for at most one hour, and routes for channels outside `DISCORD_CHANNEL_IDS` (reminder DMs) are stored only as a hash keyed by the bot token.

Website-managed GCA records hold VIDs, approvals and home overrides. The current record is kept until it is replaced or deleted. Earlier versions are kept for at most 30 days and at most 20 versions, pruned on every publish, purge and poll; the signed purge keeps only the current record, and the operator delete removes the record and all its history. Durable Object point-in-time recovery may keep earlier states for up to 30 days after a change. Nonces and write times hold no member data; expired ones are removed on later signed requests. After cutover, the `GCA_APPROVALS` and `GCA_HOME_OVERRIDES` secrets are unused copies of member data; delete them from Cloudflare while the owner keeps a current private copy for [rollback](docs/setup.md#rollback). Records stored under a previous `COORDINATOR_NAME` stay in that object; delete them before renaming.

Staff-copy cleanup is **not complete member erasure**. It leaves delivery and occurrence records, Discord messages, logs and backups intact. Likewise, erasing a member on the website and then publishing and purging removes them from the stored GCA records only: delivery and occurrence ledgers, staff copies, short-session records, Discord messages, logs and point-in-time recovery states can still hold them. There is no supported selective-erasure operation preserving the same deduplication guarantees. Disable reminders before planning broader deletion; do not manually reset their ledgers while active.

## Development

Use synthetic test data, your GitHub noreply email, and Gitleaks before committing. Preserve configured Git hooks. CI uses GitHub-hosted runners with pinned actions and minimal permissions; never supply deployment secrets or attach a private runner.

Secret scans supplement manual review. Removing a file does not erase old commits, cached pages, forks or clones.
